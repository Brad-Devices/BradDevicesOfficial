# Brad Devices — Brand Guidelines

## The mark

The Brad Devices identity is the **icon** (the `0x` mark) plus the **wordmark**. Use the SVG versions whenever possible — they scale cleanly and stay sharp at any size.

| Background | Icon | Wordmark |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Dark | `brad-devices-icon-white.svg` | `brad-devices-wordmark-white.svg` |
| Light | `brad-devices-icon-black.svg` | `brad-devices-wordmark-black.svg` |

## Clear space & sizing

- Keep clear space around the mark equal to the height of the `0x` glyph on all sides.
- Minimum display height: **24 px** for the icon, **16 px** for the wordmark.
- Do not rotate, recolor, add effects, or set the mark on a busy background.

## Color

- **Brad Gold** — the accent. Used for emphasis, receipts and highlights.
- **Dark** `#0E1117` — the primary background.
- **Light** `#F5F7FA` — the alternate background.

## Voice

- **Receipts before promises.** If a claim cannot be verified, it does not ship.
- **Specific over impressive.** "39.43 dB PSNR" beats "game-changing."
- **No invented superlatives.** "Premier," "revolutionary," "sovereign" — not our words.

## What to call things

- The fabric is **BradFusion** — never "BradNet."
- Ray tracing is **BradRT** — never "BradRays."
- The company is **Brad Devices**. The parent is **BradVerse**.

## Usage

The brand assets are provided for press, coverage and community use. Link back to <https://brad-devices.vercel.app> when you use them.
