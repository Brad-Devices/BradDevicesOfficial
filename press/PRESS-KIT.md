## The story

Festus Bradley Nyadimo was born in Kisumu, Kenya. At 15, he is designing a complete computer ecosystem — the silicon, the memory, the operating system and the software that builds them — because he believes the next great technology company can be built from Africa.

Brad Devices is not a slide deck. The software toolchain runs today: you can open the website and watch it assemble, execute and debug BradVector code in your browser. The silicon is designed to the same standard, documented in a 1,733-line unified specification, and tagged honestly — SHIPPED, DESIGNED, or VISION — so a reader always knows exactly what a file can prove.

The company's philosophy is "receipts before promises": every claim carries a tag and a link to verify it. No invented benchmarks. No pretend silicon. The receipts are the product.

---

## The two rules

1. **Two ISAs, never one.** BradISA is the CPU. BradVector is the GPU. They meet on the BradFusion fabric and share SPMP memory — they do not share instructions.
2. **Receipts before promises.** Every claim carries a tag — SHIPPED (software runs today), DESIGNED (silicon designed), VISION (honest future) — and a link to verify it.
