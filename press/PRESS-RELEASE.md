### Brad Devices: a 15-year-old from Kenya is building a complete computer ecosystem — and shipping the software first

**Kisumu, Kenya** — Brad Devices, a vertically-integrated computer company founded by 15-year-old Festus Bradley Nyadimo, today opened its public specification and released its software toolchain, which runs in any modern browser.

Brad Devices designs silicon, memory, an operating system and the software that builds them — under one roof, as one coherent plan. The company's BradVector toolchain (an assembler, interpreter, debugger and profiler) is available now and verified unattended in continuous integration. Its silicon — from the BradChip family to the BradXon flagship and the Torox GPU line — is designed and documented in a 1,733-line unified specification.

The company is built on two rules: two instruction sets, never one (BradISA for the CPU, BradVector for the GPU), and receipts before promises. Every claim on its site carries a tag — SHIPPED, DESIGNED, or VISION — and a link to verify it.

"Brad Devices exists to prove that the next great technology company can be built from Africa," said Nyadimo. "We ship the software first so the receipts come before the promises."

The company's ecosystem spans BradOS, BradCore, HyperCore and BradXon processors, Torox GPUs, the BradSense-Render neural rendering engine, and Brad Mobile Services. Its BradSense-Render M0 spatial upscaler has been measured at 39.43, 39.44 and 39.57 dB PSNR across its quality modes.

Brad Devices is self-funded and independent. The full specification, the name map and the live toolchain are available at <https://brad-devices.vercel.app>.
